# Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering is the craft of writing instructions that get reliable behaviour from a model without changing its weights.

Good prompts are specific about the task, the audience, and the output format. Giving a few worked examples, known as few-shot prompting, is often more effective than describing the format in words.

Asking the model to reason step by step before giving a final answer, sometimes called chain of thought, improves accuracy on multi-step problems. Splitting a large task into a chain of smaller prompts makes each step easier to test.

Treat prompts like code: keep them in version control, change one thing at a time, and run your evaluation set after every edit.
